A letter I should have written long ago

Dear Amma,

from your daughter, with love

When I look back at my life, I realise that so many of my memories have you quietly woven into them. The little things you did, the things you worried about, the times you stayed beside me, and the countless ways you made sure I was okay have slowly become some of the things I value most today.

I may grow older, have my own dreams, make my own choices and eventually walk through a life of my own. But there will always be a part of me that looks for your voice, your comfort and that feeling of being home. No matter how much life changes, I don't think I will ever outgrow being your daughter.

The little things

Things you may never realise

but I notice them

There are so many things you do that probably feel ordinary to you, but they have never been ordinary to me. The way you ask if I have eaten, the way you notice when something is wrong even when I don't say it, the way you worry about the smallest things and somehow always find a way to make things feel a little better.

Maybe one day I will understand the full meaning of all those little sacrifices and quiet acts of love. For now, I just want you to know that I see them. I may not always say thank you at the right time, but somewhere inside me, every little thing you have done has found a permanent place.

Things I don't say enough

If I could say everything

I would start here

I don't say “thank you” for everything. I don't always notice your tiredness, your worries or all the things you quietly put aside for me. Sometimes I get caught up in my own little world and forget that behind so many comfortable moments in my life, there is a mother who has spent years making sure I was okay.

So today, I want to say the things I should probably say more often. Thank you for your patience. Thank you for your strength. Thank you for loving me through every version of myself. And thank you for giving me a kind of love that never asked me to earn it.

Because of you

What you gave me

is more than I can explain

There are parts of me that exist because you were there to shape them. The way I care, the way I love, the way I keep going when things become difficult—somewhere in all of those things, there is a little piece of you. You taught me more through the life you lived than through anything you ever had to explain.

And when I think about what the word “home” really means, I don't think of a place. I think of a feeling. A feeling of being known, cared for and accepted even on the days when I don't have everything figured out. Somehow, you have always been that feeling for me.

A promise from your daughter

As life keeps changing

some things never will

Life will keep changing, and I know I will keep growing into someone you may not always recognise as the little girl you once held close. There will be new places, new dreams, new responsibilities and so many chapters waiting for me. But growing up will never mean leaving behind the love that brought me this far.

I hope that wherever life takes me, I will always find ways to make you proud, make you smile and give you reasons to feel that all your love was worth it. And even when I become older, somewhere deep inside, I will always be that little girl who feels safest knowing that her Amma is there.
